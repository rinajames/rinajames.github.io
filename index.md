I hold a PhD in Sociology from the University of Arizona, and have a strong background in data wrangling, advanced quantitative methods, and computational social science. I excel at data cleaning, preparation, and analysis, and in using derived data to glean new insights from existing sources; my previous work has tackled questions related to a number of pressing social issues including far-right radicalization, gun violence, and the spread of COVID-19. In my most recent project, I use Reddit post data to map user trajectories across, and churn within, far-right male supremacist communities in order to inform intervention efforts and platform policies related to extremist ideologies.

I also work closely with the Institute for Research on Male Supremacism, an intersectional feminist organization committed to analyzing and intervening in male supremacist ideologies and mobilizations, and providing support to journalists reporting on male supremacism. I have been featured as an expert on far-right male supremacist organizing in several media outlets, including *The Dublin Times*, *Vanity Fair* (forthcoming), and *The Social Eye Podcast* (forthcoming). 

Currently, I'm seeking industry and alt-ac roles that use complex data to tackle prominent social issues.
